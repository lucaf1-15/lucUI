The lucUI manifesto

Design the atmosphere.

Interfaces are not only what they do. They are the pause before the click, the confidence of a well-set label, the small glow that tells you where you are.

lucUI 2.0 is a framework for that layer: expressive foundations for teams who want their product to feel authored.
